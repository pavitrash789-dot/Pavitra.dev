# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Small business owners in India (local shops, healthcare clinics, coaching institutes, restaurants) who are not technical and need a reliable, professional website that brings genuine customer enquiries.

## Product Purpose

Give small business owners a dependable, transparently priced freelance web development service. The primary job of the site is to convert visitors into a WhatsApp enquiry or a started quote.

## Positioning

Direct, personal engagement by a dedicated developer in Kota, Rajasthan. Transparent starting prices in INR, clear deliverables, zero agency markup, and straightforward communication directly with the builder.

## Operating Context

Visitors frequently browse from mobile devices (Android) or basic laptops over Indian cellular networks. They evaluate trust rapidly through clarity of work, plain language, clear pricing, and ease of reaching out via WhatsApp.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js 14 App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, Supabase.
- Full preservation of existing dynamic pricing calculations and 17 categories.
- Supabase storage and database integrations for project requests, contact enquiries, and admin dashboard.
- File upload handling up to 10 MB per file, max 5 files.
- Lighthouse 95+ on mobile, zero layout shift, fast initial paint.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Pavitra Sharma
- Location: Kota, Rajasthan (IST)
- Voice: First person, plain, specific, warm. Sentence case. No hype words ("extraordinary", "blazing", "engineered", "bespoke", "zero surprises").
- Primary Action: "Get a quote" and "Message on WhatsApp".
- Secondary Action: "See my work".

## Evidence on Hand

- Verified portfolio project: https://my-portfolio-beta-ochre-netty7ie4y.vercel.app/
- Real geographic anchor: Kota, Rajasthan with live local time (IST).
- Real pricing models starting at ₹2,999.

## Product Principles

1. **Clarity over hype**: Speak plainly in first person. Name exact deliverables and exact prices.
2. **WhatsApp first**: Make WhatsApp conversation the lowest-friction contact path.
3. **One memorable interaction**: The merged quote builder gives immediate clarity on scope and cost.
4. **Human restraint**: Eliminate AI tropes (purple gradients, glowing cards, generic metrics, stock illustrations).

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast ratios (>= 4.5:1 for body text, >= 3:1 for large headlines), 44px+ touch targets on mobile, visible focus indicators, screen-reader friendly semantic headings and labels, and respect for `prefers-reduced-motion`.
